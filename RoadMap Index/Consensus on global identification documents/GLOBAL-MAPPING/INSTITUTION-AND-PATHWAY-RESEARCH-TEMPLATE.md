## Mandatory safeguards
1. Do not represent birth registration as an automatic grant of nationality.
2. Do not confuse a missing identity document with statelessness.
3. Do not label an institution as a nationality or refugee-status decision-maker unless the legal mandate confirms it.
4. Separate refugee status, nationality, civil identity, residence status, and possession of a document.
5. A public-facing portal or certificate verification form is not automatically an API.
6. Never place real personal case data, identity documents, biometrics, or personal identifiers in this public register.
7. Do not mark a connector as authorized or validated without institutional authorization and reproducible test evidence.
