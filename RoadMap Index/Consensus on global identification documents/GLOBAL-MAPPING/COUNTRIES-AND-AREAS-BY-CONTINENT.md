# Countries and Areas by Continent

This readable index enumerates the records in [COUNTRY-AND-JURISDICTION-REGISTER.csv](COUNTRY-AND-JURISDICTION-REGISTER.csv). It follows the United Nations Statistics Division Standard Country or Area Codes for Statistical Use (M49): https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/ .

**Inventory baseline: 248 M49 country-or-area records.** This is not a count of 248 sovereign states. It includes territories and other areas as classified by M49. Antarctica is included only as a geographic reference and is not treated as a national identity-document issuing jurisdiction.

For this project's seven-continent navigation, Central America and the Caribbean are grouped under North America. The original M49 region, subregion, M49 code, and ISO codes are retained in the CSV so the statistical source classification is not lost.
